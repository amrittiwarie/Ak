About Me

A science enthusiast turned educator — dedicated to nurturing curiosity and critical thinking.

I'm a science teacher with a Master's degree in Physics. My journey in education is driven by a deep love for the natural world and a commitment to helping students see the beauty in how the universe works — from the motion of planets to the dance of subatomic particles.

I believe in hands-on, experiment-driven teaching. Whether it's demonstrating electromagnetic induction with simple coils or explaining thermodynamics through real-world examples, I strive to make every lesson an experience, not just a lecture.

Beyond the classroom, I enjoy reading research journals, stargazing, and developing simplified study materials that make complex physics concepts accessible to all learners.
