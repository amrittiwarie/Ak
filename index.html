Teaching Philosophy

Demystifying the Cosmos & Nature

I believe science shouldn't be memorized; it should be experienced. My approach focuses on inquiry-based learning, active experimentation, and understanding the core principles that connect chemistry, physics, and biology.

  • Interactive Simulators: Engaging students with virtual experiments that reinforce theoretical concepts.
  • Real-World Context: Explaining molecular biology and physics through daily phenomena and biotechnology.
  • Critical Thinking: Teaching students how to analyze evidence, question assumptions, and design controlled trials.

Empowering the Whole Student

Academics are only one half of a student's journey. Through self-development mentoring, I guide teenagers and college students to build strong mental habits, emotional resilience, and lifelong learning techniques.

  • Habit Design: Utilizing systems from behavioral science to help students build consistent study routines.
  • Mindset Training: Cultivating a growth mindset to view obstacles as essential pathways to learning.
  • Life Goal Alignment: Helping students connect their daily academic pursuits to their broader personal goals.
